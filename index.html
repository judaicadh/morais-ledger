03 / SOURCE & METHOD

Preserving the original record

The collection

Morais’ scrapbook brings together his sermons, articles, correspondence, and other printed material. Marvin Weiner’s item descriptions provide an entry point into the collection and its historical context.

Read the original introduction ↗

What is preserved

Titles, publication names, date text, subtitles, annotations, and references are transcribed from the original archived bibliographic pages. Index headings and item references follow the original archived A–Z index. Damaged apostrophes and identified diacritics have been corrected for display. Original text is retained with each correction, and search works with or without accents. No ledger entries were imported from the replacement website.

Search coverage

Description search covers 831 ledger items. The complete original A–Z index links to scans for items 001–831. Page images are matched to the original TIFF filenames, and page labels follow Penn Libraries’ IIIF manifest.

Source trail

The requested Wayback capture is April 28, 2022. Wayback may redirect individual pages to other capture dates. Each item description retains its source URL.
